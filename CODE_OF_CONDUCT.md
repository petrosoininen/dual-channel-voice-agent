# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and inclusive. Discuss ideas and behavior rather
than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and community spaces.
Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict
participation when behavior is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's
available private contact channel. Do not include unnecessary personal information.
Maintainers will review reports as promptly and confidentially as practical.
